Intoxicated Guest Protocol

1. Stay calm and professional. Do not embarrass or argue with the guest.
2. Stop serving alcohol to the guest immediately. Inform the bartender and server discreetly.
3. Offer water, non-alcoholic beverages, and food to the guest.
4. Notify the manager or shift lead immediately.
5. Do not physically restrain the guest unless they are a direct threat to themselves or others.
6. If the guest becomes verbally aggressive, maintain a calm tone. Do not escalate. Involve the manager.
7. If the guest attempts to drive, do not block them physically. Call 911 immediately and report the vehicle, direction, and license plate if safely observable.
8. If the guest is with a sober companion, discreetly inform that person of the situation and request their assistance.
9. Offer to call a taxi, rideshare, or a sober contact on behalf of the guest.
10. If the guest becomes unconscious or shows signs of alcohol poisoning (unresponsive, slow breathing, blue lips), call 911 immediately and place them in the recovery position.
11. Document the situation: time, guest behavior, staff actions taken, and how the guest departed.
12. Do not serve alcohol to a visibly intoxicated person under any circumstances. This is a legal obligation.
