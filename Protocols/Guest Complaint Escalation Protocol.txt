Guest Complaint Escalation Protocol

1. Listen to the guest's complaint fully without interrupting. Maintain eye contact and a calm, respectful posture.
2. Acknowledge the complaint sincerely. Say: "I understand, and I'm sorry this happened. I want to make this right."
3. Do not argue, make excuses, or dismiss the complaint, even if you believe the guest is mistaken.
4. Identify the nature of the complaint: food quality, service, billing, safety, cleanliness, or staff behavior.
5. Resolve what you can immediately within your authority (replace a dish, offer a complimentary item, correct a bill).
6. If the complaint is beyond your authority or the guest is not satisfied, escalate to the manager or shift lead promptly. Do not make the guest wait more than 3 minutes.
7. The manager should approach the guest personally, introduce themselves, and engage directly.
8. Never offer a refund, discount, or compensation without manager approval unless pre-authorized.
9. Do not discuss the guest's complaint with other staff or guests in the dining area.
10. If the guest remains unsatisfied and wishes to escalate further, provide the restaurant's contact information for management follow-up.
11. After the guest leaves, document the complaint: nature, time, resolution offered, and outcome.
12. Review recurring complaints with management to identify systemic issues.
