Staff Workplace Injury Protocol

1. Ensure the injured staff member is safe and remove them from any ongoing hazard (hot surface, wet floor, sharp equipment).
2. Assess the severity of the injury calmly.
3. Call 911 immediately for: severe bleeding, burns covering large areas, suspected fractures, loss of consciousness, or eye injuries.
4. Apply basic first aid using the restaurant's first aid kit for minor injuries (small cuts, superficial burns, minor bruising).
5. Do not allow the injured staff member to continue working during the same shift if they are in pain, distressed, or medically uncertain.
6. Notify the manager or owner immediately.
7. Secure the area where the injury occurred. Do not clean it up until the manager has reviewed it.
8. Document: the time, exact location, what the staff member was doing, what caused the injury, and what first aid was applied.
9. Collect names of any witnesses.
10. Complete a workplace injury report form before end of shift and submit it to management.
11. Inform the staff member of their right to seek medical attention and workers' compensation.
12. Review the hazard or equipment involved and implement corrective action to prevent recurrence.
13. Management must report serious injuries to the relevant labor or occupational safety authority as required by law.
