Cash Handling and Register Protocol

1. Only authorized staff with register access credentials may open or operate the register.
2. Count and verify the opening till amount at the beginning of every shift. Report any discrepancy to the manager before service begins.
3. Never leave the register open and unattended.
4. Process each transaction fully before beginning the next. Do not rush or skip confirmation steps.
5. For cash payments, state the amount tendered and the change aloud before handing it back: "That's $20 out of $23.50, and $3.50 is your change."
6. Place large bills (over $20) on the register ledge until the transaction is complete before placing them in the drawer.
7. Do not keep large amounts of cash in the register drawer. Perform safe drops whenever the drawer exceeds the authorized float amount.
8. Never share register access codes or passwords with other staff. Each user must log in with their own credentials.
9. Report any suspected payment fraud, counterfeit bills, or suspicious behavior to the manager immediately.
10. Count and reconcile the register at the end of every shift. Document the closing balance and sign off.
11. Discrepancies above the tolerance threshold must be reported to management and documented before the register report is submitted.
12. Do not accept personal checks unless explicitly authorized by management policy.
13. Treat all cash handling situations as observable: assume transactions may be reviewed on camera.
