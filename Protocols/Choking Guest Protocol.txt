Choking Guest Protocol

1. Approach the guest calmly. Ask clearly: Are you choking? Can you speak or cough?
2. If the guest can cough forcefully, encourage them to keep coughing and stay with them. Do not intervene physically yet.
3. If the guest cannot speak, breathe, or cough, call 911 immediately and begin the Heimlich maneuver.
4. To perform the Heimlich maneuver:
   a. Stand behind the guest and wrap your arms around their waist.
   b. Make a fist with one hand and place it just above the navel, thumb side in.
   c. Grasp your fist with the other hand and give firm upward thrusts repeatedly until the object is dislodged.
5. If the guest loses consciousness, lower them to the floor, call 911 if not done, and begin CPR if trained.
6. Notify the manager immediately.
7. Clear the area of other guests to give space and preserve the guest's dignity.
8. Stay with the guest until emergency services arrive.
9. Document the incident fully: time, what was ordered, how the situation was resolved, staff present.
10. All front-of-house and kitchen staff should be trained in the Heimlich maneuver and CPR annually.
