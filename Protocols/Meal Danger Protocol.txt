Meal Danger Protocol

1. Stop service of the affected dish immediately.
2. Remove the item from the table and preserve it for management review.
3. Apologize to the guest and move the conversation to a calm, private tone.
4. Notify the shift lead or manager at once.
5. Document what was found, who handled it, and when it happened.
6. Check whether any other guests may have been served from the same batch.
7. Isolate related ingredients or prepared food until kitchen review is complete.
8. Offer replacement, refund, and medical help escalation if needed.
9. Do not argue, assign blame, or dispose of evidence before management review.
10. Escalate to ownership and food safety authorities if the situation requires it.
