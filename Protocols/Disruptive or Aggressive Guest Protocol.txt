Disruptive or Aggressive Guest Protocol

1. Remain calm and do not raise your voice. Speak slowly, clearly, and respectfully.
2. Do not argue, challenge, or match the guest's aggression. Acknowledge their frustration: "I understand you're upset and I want to help."
3. Notify the manager or shift lead immediately and discreetly. Do not leave a guest alone if they are becoming threatening.
4. Ask the guest to lower their voice and respect other guests and staff. Make this request once, calmly and clearly.
5. If the guest refuses and continues to disrupt the dining environment, the manager must inform the guest that they will need to leave the premises.
6. Do not physically remove or touch a guest. Only trained security personnel or law enforcement may do this.
7. If the guest becomes physically threatening or violent, call 911 immediately. Do not engage.
8. If the guest has a weapon or threatens harm, evacuate nearby staff and guests to a safe area and call 911.
9. Do not block exits or corner the guest. Keep an open path between yourself and the exit.
10. Once the guest leaves or is removed, check on the wellbeing of all nearby staff and guests.
11. Document the incident in full: time, behavior, staff actions taken, witnesses, and outcome.
12. Offer counseling or emotional support resources to any staff who were directly threatened or distressed.
