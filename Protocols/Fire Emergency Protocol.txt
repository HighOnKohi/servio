Fire Emergency Protocol

1. Remain calm and do not panic. Alert all staff immediately with a clear, loud announcement.
2. Activate the nearest fire alarm pull station if not already triggered.
3. Call emergency services (911 or local fire department) immediately.
4. Do not attempt to fight a large or spreading fire. Only use a fire extinguisher on a small, contained fire if trained to do so.
5. Guide all guests and staff to the nearest emergency exit in an orderly manner. Do not use elevators.
6. Assign a staff member to assist any guests with mobility difficulties.
7. Close doors and windows as you evacuate to slow the spread of fire and smoke. Do not lock them.
8. Proceed to the designated outdoor assembly point and account for all staff and guests.
9. Do not re-enter the building under any circumstances until the fire department gives clearance.
10. Notify the manager or owner as soon as everyone is safely evacuated.
11. Document the incident, time, cause (if known), and actions taken for insurance and reporting purposes.
12. Cooperate fully with fire investigators upon arrival.
