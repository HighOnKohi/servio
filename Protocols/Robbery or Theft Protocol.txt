Robbery or Theft Protocol

1. Do not resist or attempt to stop a robbery in progress. Your safety and the safety of guests and staff come first.
2. Comply calmly with the demands of the suspect. Do not make sudden movements.
3. Mentally note the suspect's description: height, build, clothing, hair, distinguishing features.
4. Once the suspect has left the premises, call 911 immediately.
5. Lock the entrance if it is safe to do so to preserve the scene.
6. Do not touch anything the suspect may have handled. This preserves fingerprint evidence.
7. Notify the manager or owner immediately.
8. Keep all staff and guests present and calm. Request they stay until police arrive to provide witness statements.
9. Do not speak to the media or post about the incident on social media.
10. Cooperate fully with police investigators and provide security footage if available.
11. Document everything you remember about the suspect and the sequence of events as soon as it is safe to do so.
12. Offer emotional support to any staff or guests who are distressed. Contact professional counseling support if needed.
13. Review and reinforce security procedures after the incident.
