Food Contamination Protocol

1. Remove the suspected dish from the guest's table immediately and apologize sincerely without assigning blame.
2. Do not discard the food. Seal it in a container and label it with the time, table number, and item name.
3. Pull the same batch or prep lot from service immediately until the kitchen can inspect it.
4. Notify the kitchen manager and shift lead at once.
5. Check whether any other guests have been served from the same batch or source ingredient.
6. If a guest reports illness symptoms, treat it as a medical situation and follow the Guest Medical Emergency Protocol.
7. Inspect the source ingredients for signs of spoilage, improper storage temperature, or cross-contamination.
8. Document the discovery: who found it, when, what was observed (foreign object, odor, color, texture), and what action was taken.
9. Do not allow kitchen staff to continue using any ingredient until it is cleared by the kitchen manager.
10. Conduct a full temperature log and storage review for that shift.
11. If the contamination may affect public health, notify the local food safety authority as required by law.
12. Complete a full incident report and submit it to management and ownership.
