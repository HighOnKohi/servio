Power Outage Protocol

1. Stay calm and reassure guests immediately: "We apologize for the inconvenience. We are addressing this right now."
2. Activate emergency lighting if available. Use flashlights or candles if permitted by local fire code.
3. Notify the manager or shift lead immediately.
4. Do not open refrigerators or freezers unnecessarily. A closed refrigerator maintains safe temperature for approximately 4 hours.
5. Halt all food preparation and service. Do not serve food that cannot be confirmed as safely stored.
6. If the outage is prolonged (over 2 hours), begin logging food temperatures and assess what must be discarded.
7. Contact the power utility company to report the outage and get an estimated restoration time.
8. If the outage is within the restaurant's control (tripped breaker, internal fault), notify the maintenance team.
9. Offer guests the option to wait if restoration is expected soon, or assist them in canceling their order and paying for what they have received.
10. Do not operate gas appliances if you suspect a gas leak or if gas-powered ventilation is impaired.
11. Keep all exterior exits clear and unlocked for guest safety.
12. Once power is restored, verify all equipment (refrigerators, ovens, POS systems) before resuming full service.
13. Document the outage duration, food affected, guest impact, and corrective actions taken.
