Guest Injury Protocol

1. Immediately attend to the injured guest and ask calmly: Are you in pain? Can I help you?
2. Do not move the guest if they have fallen and complain of neck, back, or head pain. Keep them still.
3. Call emergency services (911) if the injury involves: head trauma, loss of consciousness, severe bleeding, suspected fracture, or inability to stand.
4. Apply basic first aid only if you are trained. Use the restaurant's first aid kit for minor cuts or burns.
5. Notify the manager or shift lead immediately.
6. Clear the surrounding area of other guests and staff to give the injured guest space and dignity.
7. Do not admit liability, apologize in a way that implies fault, or make promises on behalf of the restaurant.
8. Document the incident in detail: time, location, what happened, names of witnesses, staff present, and actions taken.
9. Collect witness contact information if possible, with their consent.
10. Complete a formal incident report before end of shift and submit it to management.
11. Preserve any physical evidence at the scene (spilled liquid, broken item) until reviewed.
12. Follow up with management regarding any insurance or liability reporting requirements.
