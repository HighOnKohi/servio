Kitchen Safety and Hygiene Protocol

1. All kitchen staff must wash hands thoroughly with soap and warm water for at least 20 seconds before handling food, after handling raw meat, after using the restroom, and after touching their face or hair.
2. Wear clean, appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE): gloves when handling ready-to-eat food, aprons, and hair restraints at all times.
3. Use separate cutting boards and utensils for raw meat, poultry, seafood, and vegetables. Color-code boards and follow the system consistently.
4. Store raw meat below ready-to-eat food in the refrigerator at all times to prevent drip contamination.
5. Maintain refrigerator temperatures at or below 40°F (4°C) and freezer temperatures at or below 0°F (-18°C). Log temperatures twice daily.
6. Cook all proteins to safe internal temperatures: poultry to 165°F, ground meat to 160°F, whole cuts to 145°F.
7. Never leave cooked food in the temperature danger zone (40°F to 140°F / 4°C to 60°C) for more than 2 hours.
8. Sanitize all food-contact surfaces, cutting boards, and equipment before and after each use.
9. Report any signs of pest activity (droppings, damage, sightings) to management immediately. Do not attempt to handle pests yourself.
10. Do not work with food if you are experiencing symptoms of illness: vomiting, diarrhea, jaundice, or infected open wounds. Notify your manager.
11. Store chemicals and cleaning products in a separate, clearly labeled area away from all food and food equipment.
12. Dispose of waste and food scraps promptly and properly. Keep bins covered and emptied regularly.
