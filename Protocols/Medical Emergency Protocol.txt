Medical Emergency Protocol

1. Stay calm. Assess the situation quickly: Is the guest conscious? Are they breathing? Do they appear to be in severe distress?
2. Call 911 immediately if the guest shows any of: chest pain, difficulty breathing, sudden severe headache, loss of consciousness, seizure, signs of stroke (facial drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech), or uncontrolled bleeding.
3. Do not leave the guest alone. Assign one staff member to stay with them at all times.
4. Notify the manager or shift lead immediately.
5. Clear the area around the guest of other guests and unnecessary staff to provide space and dignity.
6. If the guest is unconscious and not breathing and you are CPR-trained, begin CPR immediately. Use the restaurant's AED if available.
7. If the guest is conscious, keep them calm and still. Do not give them food or water unless they specifically request it and it is clearly safe.
8. Gather information to give to emergency services: the guest's approximate age, symptoms, onset time, and any medications or allergies they mention.
9. Send a staff member to the building entrance to direct emergency responders to the exact location.
10. Do not move the guest unless they are in immediate physical danger from their surroundings.
11. After the emergency, complete a full incident report with all details, staff involved, and actions taken.
12. Ensure all staff know the location of the first aid kit and AED at all times.
